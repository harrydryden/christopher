# Corpus coverage candidates — 1 October 2026

This is a bounded source-identity review for the [SPEC §9 golden set](../../SPEC.md#9-quality-bar-and-test-plan), not a posting-label or extraction qualification. I opened first-party careers pages and followed their jobs links on 1 October 2026. The five safe additions below have been entered into `apps/worker/src/live-acceptance-manifest.ts` with `expectedRoleCount: null`; no human counted or checked every posting. A live board link can change, and an ATS adapter may still fail or return incomplete results.

The previous 25-case manifest had three primary ATS families: Greenhouse, Ashby and Eightfold. Five additions bring the current set to 30 cases and seven primary families: those three plus Lever, Workable, Teamtailor and Workday. The separate gate remains blocked: it needs eight primary ATS families, two evidenced multi-region Workday boards and a genuinely bot-protected site. HTML and JSON-LD cannot substitute for an ATS family. The two Workday pages below are source-identity evidence only; neither board's current posting locations were established by the text reader.

Datadog and Cloudflare also carry `jsHeavy` evidence. These tags rely on the retained [browser capture report](../2026-09-29/SOURCE-BROWSER-CAPTURE-REPORT.md), [source-label audit](../2026-09-29/SOURCE-LABEL-AUDIT.md), [Siemens continuation audit](../2026-09-29/implementation-evidence/source-audit/SIEMENS-CONTINUATION-LIVE.md), [Mozilla page-signal diagnostic](../2026-09-29/implementation-evidence/source-audit/mozilla-wise-netflix-page-signals.json) and [Cloudflare equivalence audit](../2026-09-29/SOURCE-EQUIVALENCE-AUDIT.md). They establish source character, not human-reviewed recall or precision. Netflix's robots restriction is not evidence of bot protection, so no `botProtected` tag was added.

The next curation pass needs one unfiltered, first-party-linked eighth ATS family; a single bounded public Workday listing observation showing locations in more than one country for each proposed Workday case; and a separately evidenced bot-protected site. Only then should the structural gate be reconsidered. Posting identity snapshots, independent human labels and the other SPEC §9 acceptance criteria remain separate work.
