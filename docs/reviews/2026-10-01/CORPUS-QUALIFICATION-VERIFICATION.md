## Posting-reference provenance and limits

The acceptance reporter already requires one result per selected case, source binding, a matching raw-file SHA-256, a complete observation and a fresh reference. A `human_reviewed` status and `completeScope: true` alone no longer qualify. Optional `reviewAttestation` now carries a non-empty reviewer name/ID, timezone-bearing `reviewedAt`, scope evidence describing the checked listing, and an explicit `fullScopeAttested` boolean. Supplied malformed metadata or review before capture is rejected; review after observation disqualifies. Historical bare human flags remain parseable but explicitly unqualified. The complete schema and review order are in [live acceptance documentation](../../live-acceptance.md#optional-posting-identity-references-schema-2). Syntax, text length and chronology cannot authenticate a person or prove that a human actually checked every listing page.

No current independently human-reviewed, full-scope posting-identity corpus has been supplied. The earlier machine-enumerated Zapier and Datadog snapshots remain diagnostics, and the early Siemens publication observation shows availability before completion, not terminal recall or precision. The 20/25 correct automatic sources at ≥0.85 with zero wrong automatic accepts was a **29 September result for the former 25-case manifest**. Five source-only cases change the selected corpus and denominator; neither 20/25 nor its 80% threshold result carries over. Fresh current-manifest discovery and posting runs are required, with one complete result and qualifying reference for every selected case.
